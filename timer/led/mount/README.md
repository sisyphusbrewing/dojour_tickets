# Ceiling hanger for the stage clock

`hanger.py` builds two STLs (needs `pip install trimesh manifold3d`):

- `hanger-tilt15.stl`: hangs the clock tipped about 15 degrees face-down toward the stage.
- `hanger-flat.stl`: hangs it straight.

Print two, as exported (lying on their side). PETG or PLA, 4 walls, 40% infill.

Hardware: 2 x M3 x 10 mm screws with washers into the panel's top two
threaded holes (the slot fits holes 8 to 42 mm below the top edge), two
S-hooks or a loop of picture wire through the lugs, two ceiling hooks, and a
separate thin safety cable from the ceiling to the panel.
