Open a card

Hold the card to the top of your phone. If it won’t tap, type the number on its back.

How to ring it in GoTab

Selling a card. Ring the “Gift card” item for the amount and take the payment. Then tap the card here and Put on that amount. No sales tax on a gift card.
Paying with a card. Tap the card, Take off the tab total (or what’s left on the card), then close the tab in GoTab with the “Gift card” payment. If the tab is more than the card, they pay the rest the usual way.

Cards

A new card: make one here, copy its link, and write the link onto a blank card with NFC Tools (Write › Add a record › URL). Then tap the card to check it.
