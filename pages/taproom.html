TAPROOM & SHOWROOM FAQ

Everything you need to know about parking, house rules, comedy shows, and planning your trip to Sisyphus.

📍
Finding the Entrance: Our street address is 712 Ontario Ave W, but our main public entrance is located around the corner on Aldrich Avenue.

Visiting & Logistics

Where do I park?

Free street parking is available along Ontario Ave and the east side of Aldrich Ave. You are also welcome to park in our customer lot behind the building or in the Dunwoody lot on Aldrich just south of Ontario (weekdays after 4pm, any time on weekends, no overnight parking). See the parking map, entrance and lot hours.

Can I get a drink before the comedy show?

Yes! The main taproom is open to the public during all regular hours, even when comedy shows are happening. We highly encourage coming early to grab a flight or a pint before the showroom doors open.

Do I need a reservation for the taproom?

No reservations are necessary! Taproom seating is completely first-come, first-served. If you have a large party or want to book the private showroom, check our Private Rentals page.

Are you wheelchair accessible?

Yes, our taproom and showroom are accessible. If you need any extra accommodations, please give us a call ahead of your visit and we’ll be happy to help.

Do you have an outdoor patio?

Yes! We have a small, humble patio available seasonally from spring through fall, weather permitting.

Food, Dogs & Taproom Fun

Can I bring my own food?

Yes, 100%. We do not operate a kitchen, so guests are encouraged to bring takeout or have DoorDash, UberEats, or local pizza delivered straight to your taproom table.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes! Well-behaved, leashed dogs are always welcome both inside the taproom and on the seasonal patio. Fresh water bowls and treats are waiting at the bar.

Are kids / families welcome?

Families and children of all ages are welcome in the general taproom. Please note, however, that all comedy showroom events feature uncensored adult stand-up and are strictly 18+.

Do you have games?

Yes! We have two full-size shuffleboard tables (free to play), four pinball machines, and a shelf of board games available for guests.

The Comedy Showroom

How does seating work in the comedy club?

Most of our shows are General Admission with first-come, first-served seating (though select special events may offer VIP seating options). We recommend arriving when doors open to grab your preferred seats together.

When do doors open for comedy shows?

Doors generally open 30 minutes before showtime. For late shows, doors typically open closer to showtime.

Can I bring my drinks into the comedy theater?

Absolutely! While no outside alcohol is permitted, you are welcome to bring any drinks purchased at the bar right into the showroom with you. You can also order drinks directly to your table during the show!

Do shows sell out?

Yes! Because our showroom is limited to just 100 seats, weekend headliners frequently sell out in advance. We strongly recommend securing your tickets online ahead of time. Please note that we do not offer a stand-by or waitlist for sold-out shows.

Open Mic & Thursday Showcase

When are the free comedy nights?

The open mic is every Tuesday at 7:00 PM in the main taproom. The showcase is every Thursday at 8:00 PM. Both are free to watch. No tickets or RSVPs, just walk in, grab a pour, and find a seat.

How do comedians get on stage?

For Tuesday, sign up online on the Open Mic page by 6:30 PM that day. A few spots are booked ahead and the rest are drawn at random before the mic starts. The Thursday showcase is booked ahead, but we save a few spots every week for first-timers: arrive by 7:30 PM and talk to the bartender. More answers in the Open Mic FAQ.
