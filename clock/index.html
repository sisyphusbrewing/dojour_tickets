The tap sticker for the bar

Staff hold their phone to this sticker and they’re clocked in or out, marked “at the bar”.

To make one: copy this link and write it onto a blank NFC sticker as a web link (the free NFC Tools app does it). Then hold your own phone to it: this page tells you if it works.

Put it where guests can’t reach, and not on metal (it won’t read). Keep the link to yourself: anyone who has it can clock in from anywhere and look like they’re at the bar.
