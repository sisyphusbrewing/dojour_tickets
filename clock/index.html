The tag at the bar

Staff hold their phone to this tag and they’re clocked in or out, marked “at the bar”.

To make one: copy this link and write it onto a blank NFC tag as a web link (the free NFC Tools app does it). Then hold your own phone to it: this page tells you if it works.

Put it where guests can’t reach, and not on metal (it won’t read). Keep the link to yourself: anyone who has it can clock in from anywhere and look like they’re at the bar.

The club wifi

The big Clock in button only works on the club wifi. Holding a phone to the tag works on any connection.

 

Tap “This is the club wifi” once, standing in the club with this phone on the wifi. If the button ever stops working for everyone at once, the internet company has given the club a new address: tap it again from the club. An iPhone with iCloud Private Relay switched on looks like it isn’t on the wifi; those phones use the tag.
