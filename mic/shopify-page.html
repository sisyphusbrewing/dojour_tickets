Two Free Comedy Nights Every Week

More stage time for comics, new ones especially. Free for everybody.

Tuesdays: Open Mic

Every Tuesday at 7:00 PM in the comedy club. Starts November 17.

  • Cost: Free, no tickets needed
  • For comics: Sign up online below. A few spots are booked ahead and the rest are drawn at random from the sign-ups before the mic starts.
  • Age: Must be 18+ to attend
  • Taproom: Open 4–9 PM on Tuesdays

The Magicians' Open Mic keeps its second Tuesday in the showroom. Two open mics, one building. Bring a friend who can't decide.

Thursdays: Free Showcase

Every Thursday at 8:00 PM in the comedy club.

  • Cost: Free, no tickets needed, no drink minimums
  • How it works: Each week one comic curates the night. They book three comics for ten minutes each: maybe a friend, someone they think is super funny, someone they think deserves a shot. One of the three closes, and each of those spots pays $20.
  • Want stage time? Sign up at the bar from 6 to 7 PM. There are open spots every week, and spots for first-timers are guaranteed.
  • Want to curate a Thursday? Email booking@sisyphusbrewing.com.
  • Age: Must be 18+ to attend
  • Taproom: Open 4–11 PM on Thursdays, so come early for a beer

Open Mic & Showcase FAQ

Do I need a ticket to watch?

No. Tuesdays and Thursdays are free to watch. Walk in, get a beer, sit down. That's the whole process.

How do I sign up for the Tuesday open mic?

Use the sign-up form above. One sign-up per email each week. Sign-ups close at 6:30 PM on the Tuesday. If you sign up after that, your name goes in for the following Tuesday.

How do names get picked on Tuesdays?

A few spots are booked ahead. Everyone else goes in the hat, and names get drawn at random right before the mic starts at 7. Be in the room to hear yours. We don't email or text the lineup, so if you're out in the car, you'll miss it.

What if my name doesn't get drawn?

Then you're in a bar on a Tuesday with free comedy. There are worse outcomes. Sign up again for next week.

How do I get on the Thursday showcase?

Sign up at the bar from 6 to 7 PM that Thursday. Each week's curator also books three comics for ten minutes each, paid $20 a spot. If you'd like to curate a Thursday yourself, email booking@sisyphusbrewing.com.

I've never done stand-up. Where do I start?

Either night works. On Tuesday, sign up above and check the first-time box so the host knows. On Thursday, there's a sign-up sheet at the bar. It's out from 6 to 7 PM, and first-timers are guaranteed a spot.

What time do things start?

The Tuesday open mic starts at 7:00 PM in the comedy club. The Thursday showcase starts at 8:00 PM. We're closed Thanksgiving, so there's no Thursday showcase on November 26, December 24 or December 31.

What about the Magicians' Open Mic?

The Magic Underground runs the Magicians' Open Mic on the second Tuesday of every month at 7:00 PM in the showroom. It's a separate show with its own tickets. The free stand-up mic still happens out in the taproom that night.

Is there an age limit?

Yes. You have to be 18 or older to perform or watch.

Can I bring food?

Yes. We don't have a kitchen, so bring takeout or have something delivered to your table. Drinks come from our bar, and outside alcohol isn't allowed.
