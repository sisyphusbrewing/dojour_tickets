Two Free Comedy Nights Every Week

More stage time for comics, new ones especially. Free for everybody.

Tuesdays: Open Mic

Every Tuesday at 7:00 PM in the main taproom. Starts November 17.

  • Cost: Free, no tickets needed
  • For comics: Sign up online below. A few spots are booked ahead and the rest are drawn at random from the sign-ups before the mic starts.
  • Age: Must be 18+ to attend
  • Taproom: Open 4–9 PM on Tuesdays

The Magicians' Open Mic keeps its second Tuesday in the showroom. Two open mics, one building. Bring a friend who can't decide.

Thursdays: Free Showcase

Every Thursday at 7:00 PM in the comedy club, starting November 19.

  • Cost: Free, no tickets needed, no drink minimums
  • The lineup: Booked ahead with the best comics in town
  • First time on stage? We save a few spots every week for first-timers. Arrive by 6:30 PM and talk to the bartender.
  • Age: Must be 18+ to attend
  • Taproom: Open 4–11 PM on Thursdays, so come early for a beer

Thursday, November 12 is the last night in the old open mic format: sign-up 6–7 PM, list posted at 7:15 PM, show at 8 PM.

Getting here
