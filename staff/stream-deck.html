Stream DeckHow the showroom buttons work
 ‹ Staff

Eight buttons, four knobs, one show. You push buttons. The computer in the office does the rest.

The basics

The Stream Deck at the bar runs sound, the record button, the stage timer, the hallway lights and the menus. It's a remote control: the brains are on the office PC (a program called Companion), and the mixer is in the office too.

The deck has three pages: SHOW, BAR and TIMER. The purple, blue or green key in the top-left corner says which page you're on. Press it to go to the next one. Swiping sideways on the touch strip also changes page.

The four knobs never change. Whatever page you're on, they're always MIC 1, MIC 2, MUSIC and GOD MIC. Turn for louder or quieter, push to mute. The strip above each knob shows its name and level, and turns red with MUTED when it's off. Tap the strip to unmute.

The overall room volume is set once on the mixer and never lives on a knob. If the whole room is too loud or too quiet, see If something's wrong.

  • Show page

    You're on SHOW. Press for the BAR page.

  • Record video

    Starts recording the set on the office PC (cameras plus room sound). The key turns red and shows the running time. Press again to stop. Orange means paused.

  • Reset sound

    Puts the mixer back to the saved show mix. Use it when sound is weird: a mic dead, music gone, everything too quiet. Safe to press any time; it just undoes whatever got bumped.

  • Mic 1 · Mic 2 · Music · God mic

    Green ON means live. Press to mute: the key goes red and says MUTED. Press again to bring it back. Same thing as pushing the knob below it.

  • Timer page

    You're on TIMER. Press to go back to SHOW.

  • Start 5 / 7 / 10 / 15 min

    Starts the comic's clock (counting up from 0:00) on the stage timer screen. Press it when the comic hits the stage. Pressing another one restarts at the new length.

  • Add 1 min

    Gives the comic one more minute. Press it as many times as you're feeling generous.

  • Stop timer

    Clears the timer. Press it when the comic is off.

  • Wrap it up

    Puts WRAP IT UP on the stage clock right now; press again to clear. The clock already goes red at 1 minute left and says WRAP IT UP on its own at 30 seconds. Over time it says "You better know what you are doing....", and a minute over, "No seriously.... are you famous?"

A show night
  1. Doors. BAR page: Hall lights pre-show. Start the pre-show playlist in Spotify on the office PC and set it with the MUSIC knob.
  2. Host walks up. SHOW page: check MIC 1 is green. Turn MUSIC down or push the knob to mute it. Press Record video.
  3. Each comic. TIMER page: start their time. Stop timer when they're off.
  4. Announcements from the office: GOD MIC on, talk, mute it again.
  5. After the show. SHOW page: press Record video to stop (the key goes back to dark). Music back up. BAR page: hall lights to whatever suits the rest of the night.
If something's wrong
  • Sound is weird (a mic dead, music gone, too quiet): SHOW page, Reset sound. Fixes most things.
  • A key is red. That thing is muted. Press it.
  • The deck is blank or frozen. The office PC probably restarted. Follow If the show computer restarts. It takes about two minutes.
  • No music even after Reset sound: the office PC's sound must stay on the headphone jack. Don't change it or unplug the USB cable to the mixer.
  • A hall light key does nothing. Those go through the internet. Wait a minute and press again.
  • Still stuck. Call Sam. Then improvise; you work at a comedy club.
