Stream DeckHow the showroom buttons work
 ‹ Staff

Two screens, four knobs, one show. You push buttons. The computer in the office does the rest.

The basicsNew layout, Nov 13

The Stream Deck at the bar runs the lights, the sound, the music, the recording and the comic's clock. It's a remote control: the brains are on the office PC.

The top row is the night, left to right: DAYTIME, PRE-SHOW, START SHOW, ALL OFF. The one that's on is lit and says on now.

The deck changes screens by itself. Press START SHOW and it becomes the SHOW screen. Press END SHOW and it goes back to HOME. There is no NEXT PAGE key any more.

The four knobs never change: MIC, MUSIC, LIGHTS, CLOCK. Turn for more or less. Push for on/off. The square on the touch screen above each knob says what it's doing; tap that square for more of that thing, and BACK (top left) to return.

Gone on purpose: the RECORD key (recording starts with START SHOW and stops with END SHOW), anything you had to hold down, and the alarm (use the keypad or the SimpliSafe app).

  • Daytime

    Regular taproom hours. Hallway soft warm white, poster lamp and shuffleboard on, room lights full, candles off.

  • Pre-show

    Doors are open. Hallway in show colours, the Sisyphus sign, wall wash, poster lamp and shuffleboard on, candles on, stage lights blue, room lights down to 20%.

  • Start show

    One press starts the show: room lights fade out, stage lights go to the show look, the stage mic is switched on, the recording starts, and the deck moves to the SHOW screen.

    On a show night this key also counts the guests who haven't checked in at the door yet: 8 PM SHOW, 31 TO COME, then ALL HERE. The number comes from the door app and catches up within half a minute. After the early show it counts the late show. No number means no show tonight, or the deck can't reach the door list; the key works the same either way.

  • All off

    Closing. Hallway, sign, wall wash, plugs, room lights, candles and stage lights all off, and the comic's clock is cleared. The hallway runs over the internet, so if it doesn't answer, press again in a minute.

  • God mic

    The announcer's mic in the office. Tap to talk: the key turns green and says GOD MIC IS LIVE. Tap again when you're done.

  • Next song

    Skips to the next song in Spotify.

  • Print 5 paper menus

    Prints five copies of today's taps on the office printer, straight from GoTab. Give it a moment.

  • Refresh TV menu

    Makes the taproom TV reload the beer list. Press it after a keg change in GoTab.

  • Show is on

    Red with REC and a running time means the show is being recorded. If it is orange and says NOT RECORDING, tap it once.

  • End show

    Asks END THE SHOW? first. YES END SHOW brings the lights up to the pre-show look, stops the recording and the clock, and takes the deck back to HOME. NO GO BACK changes nothing.

  • Start clock

    Press it when the comic hits the stage. It starts the comic's clock on the stage timer screen at the length shown above the CLOCK knob. While a clock is running the key says RESTART CLOCK: press it when the next comic walks up.

  • Wrap it up

    Puts WRAP IT UP on the comic's screen right now. Tap again to clear it. The clock already goes red at 1 minute left and says WRAP IT UP on its own at 30 seconds.

  • Stop clock

    Clears the comic's clock. Press it when the comic is off.

  • God mic

    Same as on HOME: tap to talk, tap again when you're done.

The four knobsSame on HOME and SHOW
  • Mic

    The stage mic. Turn for volume. Push for on/off: the square goes red and says MIC IS OFF. Tap the square for all four mics.

  • Music

    Turn for volume. Push to play or pause Spotify. Tap the square for next and last song.

  • Lights

    The room lights over the tables and the bar, together. Turn to dim or brighten, push for on/off. Tap the square for stage lights, candles and each dimmer on its own.

  • Clock

    The comic's clock. Turn to set the length, a minute a click. Push to start; push again to stop. While it runs the square shows the time, goes red when the light is on, and says WRAP IT UP and OVER TIME when the comic's screen does.

A show night
  1. Doors. Press PRE-SHOW. Push the MUSIC knob to start the playlist and turn it to taste.
  2. Waiting on the room. The START SHOW key counts down the guests still to come, so you can see from the bar when the room is in.
  3. Host walks up. Press START SHOW. Lights drop, the stage mic is on, the recording starts. Push the MUSIC knob to pause the music.
  4. Each comic. Set their time with the CLOCK knob, then START CLOCK when they hit the stage. STOP CLOCK when they're off.
  5. Announcements from the office: GOD MIC, talk, tap it again.
  6. After the show. END SHOW, then YES. Lights come up and the recording stops. Push the MUSIC knob to bring the music back. On a two-show night, do it all again for the late show.
  7. Last one out. ALL OFF, then set the alarm at the keypad.
If something's wrong
  • You're on a screen you don't recognise. Press BACK, top left.
  • The MIC square is red. The stage mic is off. Push the MIC knob.
  • SHOW IS ON is orange. It isn't recording. Tap it once.
  • Sound is weird (a mic dead, music gone, too quiet): tap the MIC square, then RESET SOUND. Fixes most things.
  • A little warning triangle in the corner of some keys. Something those keys talk to didn't answer last time. The keys still work. If a light or the candles didn't change, press again.
  • A key says PLUG IN DECK. The deck's battery is low. Plug it in.
  • No guest count on START SHOW on a show night. The key still starts the show; check the door app for who's in.
  • The deck is blank or frozen. The office PC probably restarted. Follow If the show computer restarts. It takes about two minutes.
  • No music even after Reset sound: the office PC's sound must go to OUT 3-4 (BEHRINGER X-AIR), over the USB cable to the mixer. Don't unplug that cable. If Spotify is quiet, restart it.
  • A hall light does nothing. Those go through the internet. Wait a minute and press the look again.
  • Still stuck. Call Sam. Then improvise; you work at a comedy club.
