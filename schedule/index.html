The usual week

“Fill the usual week” puts these shifts on the week you’re looking at as drafts. A day that already has a shift is left alone, and a shift is left open when its person has that day off.

To change the usual week, get a week the way a normal one should look, then save it here.

How many people a day needs

The Schedule reads the show calendar and works out each day’s number from these. Change them whenever they’re wrong.

A day that runs longer than 8 hours counts twice: an opener and a closer. A free event on the same night as a ticketed show doesn’t add again. For one odd day (a holiday, a private party), tap that day’s number in the week.

Days I can’t work

Tap the days you can never work. For a one-off, ask for time off instead.

How to reach me

For a new schedule, a change to your shift, a shift up for grabs, an answer on time off, and a reminder the morning you work.

Texts aren’t switched on yet. Until they are, a text is sent as an email, so add your email too.

Put my shifts on my calendar

Your posted shifts show up in your phone’s calendar and stay up to date by themselves: a new shift, a change or a swap follows along. Tap once.

Getting your link…

iPhone or Mac calendar Google Calendar

iPhone: tap the first button, then Subscribe, then Add. Android: tap Google Calendar, then Add; if your phone opens the Calendar app instead, open this page on a computer once and do it there, and it shows on your phone too. Google checks for changes every few hours, Apple about every hour, so the Schedule here is always the latest word. Outlook or anything else: copy the link and add it as a calendar “from the internet”.

The link is yours alone; anyone with it can see your shifts. Shared it by mistake? and the old one stops working.
