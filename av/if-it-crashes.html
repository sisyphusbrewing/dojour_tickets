If the show computer restarts

Office PC: runs the Stream Deck, recording and pre-show music. The mixer keeps its own settings.

  1. Press the power button on the PC. Wait 2 minutes. It signs in by itself and opens Companion, OBS, Spotify and X AIR Edit.
  2. Check the Stream Deck at the bar. It should show the SHOW page. If it stays blank, open Companion from the Start menu on the PC and wait 30 seconds.
  3. Music: in Spotify, press play on the pre-show playlist. Turn it up or down with the MUSIC knob on the Stream Deck.
  4. Sound seems wrong (too quiet, a mic dead, music missing): press RESET SOUND on the Stream Deck's SHOW page. It puts the mixer back to the saved show mix.
  5. Recording: press RECORD on the Stream Deck. It turns red and counts up while recording.

Please don't

  • Unplug the USB cable between the PC and the mixer (it carries the mics to the recording).
  • Change the PC's sound output. It must stay on the headphone jack, which feeds the mixer's AUX IN.
