What the numbers mean

Spent is what the ads cost so far, out of the budget you agreed to.

People reached is how many different people saw an ad at least once.

Clicks to tickets counts people who tapped through to the ticket page, not every tap on the ad.

Cost per click is spent divided by those clicks. Lower is better.

Facebook and Instagram report a day late, so their numbers trail Reddit's. Ticket counts are the ones you, the club or Punchup gave us.
